Garage Door Cable Replacement

Garage door cables work together with the springs to safely raise and lower your garage door. Frayed, stretched, or broken cables can cause uneven movement, create safety hazards, and place additional stress on the rest of the lifting system.

Because garage door cables operate under significant tension, repairs should always be handled by trained professionals using the proper equipment and replacement components.
